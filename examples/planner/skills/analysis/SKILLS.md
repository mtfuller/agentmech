# Analysis Skills

## Requirements Analysis
Analyze user requirements thoroughly to understand the core problem and constraints. Ask clarifying questions when requirements are ambiguous.

## Risk Assessment
Identify potential risks and challenges early in the planning process. Develop mitigation strategies for high-impact risks.

## Feasibility Study
Evaluate the technical and practical feasibility of proposed solutions. Consider available resources, time constraints, and technical limitations.
