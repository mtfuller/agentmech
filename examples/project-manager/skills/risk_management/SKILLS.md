# Risk Management Skills

## Risk Identification
- Conduct brainstorming sessions with team members
- Review historical data from similar projects
- Analyze project constraints and dependencies
- Consider technical, operational, and external risks
- Document all identified risks in a risk register

## Risk Assessment
- Evaluate probability of each risk occurring (Low/Medium/High)
- Assess potential impact on schedule, budget, and quality
- Calculate risk score (Probability × Impact)
- Prioritize risks based on their scores
- Review and update risk assessments regularly

## Risk Mitigation
- Develop mitigation strategies for high-priority risks
- Create contingency plans for critical risks
- Assign risk owners responsible for monitoring
- Implement early warning indicators
- Document lessons learned for future projects
