# Communication Skills

## Stakeholder Communication
- Identify communication needs for each stakeholder group
- Establish regular communication schedules (daily standups, weekly updates)
- Use appropriate channels (email, meetings, reports)
- Tailor message content and format to audience
- Document key decisions and action items
- Follow up on commitments and deliverables

## Status Reporting
- Provide clear, concise project status updates
- Include progress against milestones and deliverables
- Highlight accomplishments and completed work
- Report on key metrics (schedule variance, budget status)
- Identify blockers and risks proactively
- Propose solutions, not just problems

## Meeting Facilitation
- Prepare clear agenda with objectives and time allocations
- Start and end meetings on time
- Keep discussions focused and productive
- Encourage participation from all attendees
- Capture action items with owners and due dates
- Distribute meeting notes within 24 hours
